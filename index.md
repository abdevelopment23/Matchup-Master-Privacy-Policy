The app functions entirely offline and does not require an active internet connection to operate. No data is ever sent or received over the internet.

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, crash-reporting tools, advertising networks, or external SDKs.

Data Security
Because your data is stored strictly and locally on your physical device, you maintain total control over it. If you delete the app, your saved lineups will be permanently removed from your device.
